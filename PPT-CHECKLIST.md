# CENTAURA — essential competition presentation

Use these 12 slides. Give each one a clear headline and a strong visual.

1. **Brand introduction:** CENTAURA logo, tagline and one hero product image.
2. **Core problem:** buyers cannot sense a fragrance's character from an online bottle image. Connect directly to The Scent You Can See brief.
3. **Target audience:** choose one primary buyer, their occasion, need and purchase barrier. Explain why this audience wants the collection.
4. **Purpose and positioning:** brand promise, premium positioning and meaningful differentiation.
5. **Four fragrances:** Kasturi Attar, Mitti Attar, Mysore Sandalwood and Mogra; character and Indian scent associations.
6. **Aura Language:** explain how colour, form, texture, motion and typography express each scent. Show Mitti versus Mogra as one clear comparison. Explain your design choices.
7. **Visual identity:** logo, palette values, typefaces and consistent rules across the collection.
8. **Product and packaging:** bottles, cartons, discovery set and proposed sizes. Show coherent presentation.
9. **AuraWear innovation:** explain the proposed fragrance bracelet, filled replaceable pod and personalisation. Show how it would be worn, filled or replaced and release scent. State that the mechanism requires prototyping; do not claim tested performance.
10. **Deliverables board:** 10–12 clearly labelled actual designs, including packaging, campaign, gifting, discovery, website and relevant touchpoints.
11. **Advertising and communication:** one campaign idea, primary audience, message, channel choices, call to action and proposed success measures. Include a short website demo.
12. **Growth and impact:** staged launch plan, pricing rationale, sampling and repeat purchase plan, proposed refill goals and measurable targets. Identify assumptions requiring validation.

## Final rehearsal checklist

- Put perfume and visual scent translation at the centre of the pitch.
- Explain the bracelet as an extension of that fragrance system.
- Label indicative pricing, proposed targets and unvalidated physical features honestly.
- Keep screenshots and a PDF export as backup; rehearse the local website on the judging laptop.
- Confirm the organiser's presentation time and required submission formats.
